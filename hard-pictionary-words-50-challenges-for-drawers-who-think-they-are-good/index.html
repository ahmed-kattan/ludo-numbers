Hard pictionary words: 50 challenges for drawers who think they are good

Ahmed Moaz · 20 October 2026

Some groups outgrow the easy words in an evening. They draw a house in four seconds and a dog in six, and the game stops being funny. For them, hard pictionary words are the cure. The words below are chosen because the picture is not obvious: they are abstract, compound or dependent on a clever symbol. Use them in a draw and guess round on paper, or add them as custom words in an online table with friends.

What makes a word hard?

Three things. The word has no single image (freedom, boredom). It has two or more parts that must both appear (drawbridge, shoelace). Or its meaning depends on context, and a drawing alone cannot say which. A hard word asks the drawer to choose a symbol and trust the table to follow.

How do you draw a hard word?

Think in symbols. Freedom is an open cage. Silence is a finger on lips. A close call is a car and a very thin gap. Choose the symbol that most people will read the same way. If the table is slow, add a second clue: an arrow, a word bubble or a tiny scene.

How should the clock be set?

Hard words need time. Use 120 seconds and three hints for a first game, then tighten the clock as the table improves. In a scored game, remember that guesses arrive later on hard words, so points are lower for everyone. The scoring still rewards the quickest guess, and the drawer is paid from how fast the table read the drawing, so a clear symbol pays.

Can you play it online?

Yes. On Boardit, a free site I make, you can set the drawing time to 120 seconds, three hints and your own custom list for a hard round. It is an online drawing game for two to twelve, so a table of experts can play with friends in other cities.

How do you score a hard round fairly?

Hard words produce long silences, so change the scoring. Give a point to the drawer for every word the table solves, whatever the time, and a point to the first guesser. Add a bonus for a word solved in under thirty seconds. A table of four playing ten hard words can finish in about thirty minutes, and the totals will be low, which is the right feeling: low scores mean the words were hard.

It also helps to run a warm-up of five easy words so that the drawers have their hands in. A cold table struggles with abstract words in the first minute, and a bad start spoils the mood.

When should you pass?

If a word truly has no picture, a drawer may ask for a different one. In a paper game, agree a rule: one pass per round. In a table with a choice of words, the drawer simply picks the easiest of the offered ones.

A closing challenge

Try the whole list once, with a three-minute timer for each, and score a point for every word the table gets. Fifty words, twenty points is a very good night.
