New Year's Eve games that end before midnight: which of six are safe to start at 11 pm

Ahmed Moaz · 4 November 2026

A game that ends at 12:03 is a game that ruined the countdown. Some games are easy to time and some are not. Before you pick New Year's Eve games, ask one question: how far can this game's length wander? Here is how six games rank, from the surest bet to the one to leave for January.

Why does a game's length wander?

Because a game has two sources of delay. The first is the rules: how many turns it needs. The second is people: arguments, snacks, a slow connection. Rules you can measure. People you can only plan around.

Midnight is also fixed. Tradition says "Auld Lang Syne", the song Robert Burns sent to the Scots Musical Museum in 1788, is sung at the stroke of midnight on Hogmanay. Whatever you play before it has to end with minutes to spare.

The ranking, from steadiest to most wandering

I ranked the six games on Boardit, my own site, because they are the ones whose rules I can measure. All six are free, and there is no download.

  1. Trivia Tavern. The length is built in: its listing is 14 minutes or so, and everyone answers at once, so no single player can slow it down.
  2. Quick Ludo. The host picks two pieces each, and a game lasts about five minutes. Ludo with four pieces carries a listing of 30 to 60 minutes, far too wide for a night with a deadline.
  3. Draw and Guess. You can compute the longest case. Eight players, three rounds and 100 seconds a drawing is 24 drawings, or 40 minutes. Change the settings to four players, two rounds and 40 seconds and the worst case is 320 seconds, under six minutes.
  4. Who's the Spy. The listing says 20 minutes, but a vote and a runoff can stretch it. Fix a hard stop.
  5. Snakes and Ladders. The listing says 10 to 25 minutes, which looks fine, but the luck is wide. Model the Classic board and one lone counter averages 39.23 throws to finish, with a median of 32. For that one counter, one game in ten takes more than 72 throws, and one in a hundred takes more than 128. Four players are expected to play roughly 19 rounds before someone finishes, yet some games run much longer.
  6. Property Rush. Nothing in the rules counts rounds; the table plays until a single player is left solvent. The listing says 45 minutes, but nothing caps it. A smaller starting purse such as $500 will shorten it, though it gives no guarantee. Save it for another night.

The 11 pm test

A simple rule: only start a game at 11 pm if you can state its worst case and it fits before 11:50. By that rule, trivia, Quick Ludo and a trimmed drawing round pass. The rest wait.

If the host wants a longer game, start it earlier. Anything with a wide spread should begin at 9 or 10 pm, with an agreement to stop it where it stands.

Does a stopping rule spoil the game?

Not if you say it first. "We stop at 11:50 wherever we are" is a rule like any other, and players adjust. In a luck game, the leader at 11:50 wins. In a bluffing game, the host can end the round and reveal the spy. Nobody feels cheated if the rule was announced.

For Snakes and Ladders, avoid the first-to-3 match on a deadline night. The host may switch it on, but it multiplies the uncertainty, and a single game is enough.

What fills the gap before the countdown?

Keep the final ten minutes empty, or fill them with something that has no end condition: a speech, a toast, a slow round where each person names a high point of the year. Many offices and families like a quiet minute before the noise.

Other traditions carry on after midnight anyway. The song is sung and the glasses clink, and nobody asks who won.

If you are on a call

Keep the video call open and play in a browser alongside it. The host should watch the clock and announce the stop, because games do not know it is New Year's Eve. This works as a game night for friends in different places, since each person only needs a name to join. Short party games online help everyone reach midnight together.

A tip for next year

Start a day earlier. Try Property Rush on the evening before, and keep the last hour for the quick ones. The deadline will still be there, but the party will be easier.
