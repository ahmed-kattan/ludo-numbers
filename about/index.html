About

I am Ahmed Moaz. I build Boardit, a small site of free browser games that includes Ludo, so I spend a lot of time with the rules engine and with simulated games.

This blog is where I write up what those simulations show about Ludo: luck and skill, captures, game length and seat order. Every figure says how many games it comes from and what the simulation cannot tell you.

Corrections are welcome. If a number here looks wrong, the method section of each note says how to check it.

Written by Ahmed Moaz. Figures labelled as simulated come from computer play, not from people.
