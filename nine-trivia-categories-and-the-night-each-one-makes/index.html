Nine trivia categories and the night each one makes

Ahmed Moaz · 9 November 2026

Trivia Tavern has nine categories and a mixed setting. They are a good way to decide what kind of night you want. This is how I think about each.

A night of general knowledge

The all-rounder. Questions come from everywhere, so nobody has a clear edge. If you are unsure, start here.

A night of entertainment

Films, television and celebrities. Best for a casual group that likes talking about what they watch. Expect arguments about the cast.

A night of music

Songs, bands and artists. It suits a mixed-age table, because everyone has a decade they know. Expect people to hum.

A night of sports

For a group that follows a league or two. It can leave non-fans out, so pair it with something else.

A night of geography

Capitals, rivers, borders. A good category for a table with travellers, and for children who are learning.

A night of history

Dates and events. It works well with the closest-number question at the end of every round, where the answer is often a year.

A night of science and nature

Animals, space, the body. Children often do well here, and it makes a table feel clever.

A night of food and drink

Everyone eats. A friendly pick for a family evening.

A night of video games

For a group of players. It is a fine second category and a rough first one for a mixed table.

A night of mixed

A bit of everything. It is the safe choice, and the one I use when I do not know the room.

Where to play

Boardit's Trivia Tavern is a free quiz for two to eight players, and the questions are about well-known things. The host picks the categories in the lobby.

A way to choose

If the group cannot agree, put three categories to a vote and let Mixed break a tie. A night built from two categories the table chose feels more personal than one that was picked for it, and it takes less than a minute to settle.

How big each category is

The categories are not the same size. General knowledge holds 387 questions, Sports 336, Science and nature 315 and Geography 311, with Entertainment the largest at 491 and Food and drink the smallest at 142. A narrow night in the small categories repeats sooner, so mix in a bigger one when you play often.

Picking one

For a first night, mixed. For a second night, ask the group which two they want and play those.
