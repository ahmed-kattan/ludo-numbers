Star Wars trivia questions: 10 on Jedi, Sith and the galaxy far, far away

Ahmed Moaz · 19 October 2026

A galaxy far, far away has been a quiz favourite for decades. These ten Star Wars trivia questions cover heroes, villains and machines, each answered with a note and checked against a named source. Use them at a film night or as a round in a trivia quiz online with friends who know their lightsabers.

The questions

  1. What colour is Darth Vader's lightsaber?
  2. What kind of creature is Chewbacca in Star Wars?
  3. Who created Star Wars?
  4. Which actor played Luke Skywalker?
  5. What is the name of the moon-sized space station destroyed by Luke Skywalker?
  6. What is the name of the cute green baby nicknamed Baby Yoda in The Mandalorian?
  7. Which Star Wars film first introduced Darth Vader's famous reveal to Luke?
  8. Which actor played Obi-Wan Kenobi in the Star Wars prequels?
  9. What is Kylo Ren's real name in Star Wars?
  10. What are the good guys' mystical powers called in Star Wars?

The answers, with a note on each

  1. Red. Sith lightsabers glow red because, in the story, their crystals have been bled.
  2. A Wookiee. Chewbacca's roar was made from recordings of bears, walruses, lions and other animals.
  3. George Lucas, who sold Lucasfilm, and Star Wars with it, to Disney in 2012 for about 4 billion dollars.
  4. Mark Hamill, who is also the voice of the Joker in many Batman cartoons and games.
  5. The Death Star. Luke destroys the first one by hitting a small exhaust port.
  6. Grogu, whose name was only revealed in the show's second season.
  7. The Empire Strikes Back. The reveal was kept so secret that the actor was told a different line.
  8. Ewan McGregor, whose uncle, Denis Lawson, played Wedge Antilles.
  9. Ben Solo, the son of Han Solo and Leia.
  10. The Force. Jedi was listed as a religion by over 390,000 people in a census.

How hard is the set?

Questions 1 to 4 are for anyone. Questions 5 to 8 are medium and suit fans. Questions 9 and 10 are the kind that start an argument about which films count. A friendly rule is to accept any of the characters' common names as an answer.

How do you use it on the night?

Place the Star Wars set at the end of a trivia night as a bonus round, and use it to break ties among the teams that have been level all night.

Why is the Death Star such a favourite?

Because the weakness is so small. A moon-sized station falls to one shot at an exhaust port, and every fan knows the line. A quizmaster who asks the table how big the port was gets a flurry of guesses. It is a lovely example of a story detail that has outgrown its film and become part of the language.

How do you plan a Star Wars night?

Watch one film, quiz through the interval and let the winners choose the next. Divide guests into light and dark, and give them lightsaber colours as team names. For children, stage a duel with cardboard tubes and score points for sound effects. A themed night gives the quiz a place in the evening, and the table does the rest.

What follow-up questions work?

Ask who is Anakin Skywalker's Sith master, which Star Wars film introduced Han Solo and who played Princess Leia. A table of fans will have its own, and the bank has a few more. A final question is always the same: which film is the strongest? Take a show of hands.
